# Open Questions — for qualified professionals

Questions that the Launch Team drafts deliberately did **not** answer,
because answering them requires a qualified Pakistani lawyer (or other
professional) rather than an assumption. Each item says where it came
from. Nothing in the drafts should be published as final until the
relevant item is resolved.

## Privacy (from `docs/legal/privacy.md`)

1. **Breach notification duty.** If TalentBridge suffers a personal-data
   breach, must it notify a Pakistani authority? Which authority, in what
   form, and within what timeframe? (The drafts promise to notify affected
   users; the authority question is open.) Pakistan's data-protection
   legislation status must be confirmed as at the review date.
2. **Retention vs. deletion.** Does any Pakistan law or regulation impose
   a **minimum** retention period on job-application / employment records
   that would override a candidate's deletion request? If so, which
   records and how long? (The drafts state 24-month application retention
   as a policy choice and promise deletion within 30 days of an approved
   request.)
3. **Essential cookies.** For a site using essential/authentication
   cookies only (no advertising or analytics cookies), is any consent
   banner or specific disclosure legally required in Pakistan?
4. **Minimum age.** The drafts state the platform is for users aged 16+.
   Is 16 the correct threshold under applicable Pakistan law for an
   employment platform?
5. **Cross-border storage.** Data is hosted with providers whose data
   centres may be outside Pakistan. Are there any current restrictions or
   disclosure duties on that for this kind of platform?

## Scam-job policy (from `docs/legal/scam-policy.md`)

6. **Identity documents.** At what point in a Pakistan hiring process is
   it legitimate for an employer to request a candidate's CNIC copy or
   bank details (e.g. only after a written offer / for payroll setup)?
   The scam policy's "keep hiring on the platform" rule needs this line
   drawn precisely.
7. **Incorporation into terms.** How should the scam-job policy be
   incorporated into the employer terms of use so the consequences ladder
   (warning → removal → suspension → termination) is enforceable?
8. **Reporting crime.** When a report indicates a completed fraud (money
   already taken), should the platform proactively refer the reporter to
   a specific authority (e.g. FIA cybercrime reporting), and is the
   platform itself obliged to report?

## Appeal process (from `docs/legal/appeal-process.md`)

9. **Response-rate exposure.** Does publishing an organisation's response
   rate and "No response" marks create defamation or unfair-competition
   exposure in Pakistan, and is the documented appeal route (contact page,
   acknowledge in 2 business days, decide in 5) sufficient mitigation?
10. **Contractual weight of SLAs.** Do the acknowledgement/decision
    timelines stated in the drafts create binding commitments the
    platform must staff for, and should they be softened ("aim to")
    before publication?
